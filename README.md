
# My Favorite Movie: Always Yours, Never Mine

## About the Movie
*Always Yours, Never Mine* is a Filipino romantic drama starring Joshua Garcia and Julia Barretto. It tells the story of Apollo and Luna, two people whose lives keep crossing paths over thirty years. Their story explores love, timing, choices, and destiny.

## My Favorite Characters
- **Apollo** – A man who continues to encounter love and unexpected challenges.
- **Luna** – A woman whose life and choices are connected to Apollo's story.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it shows that love is not always about perfect timing. It has an emotional story, interesting characters, and meaningful lessons about relationships and life.

## My Favorite Part
> Sometimes, love means making difficult choices and accepting what life brings.
